# NERC PRC-029-2 in this package

This page records what `ridethrough/prc029.py` carries, where each value
comes from, and the one reading that went into storing the tables.

The tables and constants are carried as data and checked against the
standard. Nothing in this package grades an inverter-based resource yet.

## Source

NERC Reliability Standard PRC-029-2, "Frequency and Voltage Ride-through
Requirements for Inverter-Based Resources", adopted by the NERC Board of
Trustees on 18 August 2026 (version history, page 13). The values were read
from the 18 page PDF in NERC's standards library, downloaded on
28 November 2026. Its sha256 is recorded at the top of
`validate/src_prc_029_2.txt`.

The standard applies to Generator Owners of Bulk Electric System
inverter-based resources, and of non-BES inverter-based resources with an
aggregate nameplate capacity of 20 MVA or more connected to a common point
of connection at 60 kV or more (Section 4).

The effective date is in the Implementation Plan for PRC-029-2, which this
page does not cover.

## Where the standard measures

| What | Rule | Clause |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voltage | At the high-side of the main power transformer | R1, R2 |
| Applicable voltage | The max or min of phase-to-neutral or phase-to-phase fundamental RMS voltage at that point | Attachment 1, item 5 |
| Per-unit base | Nominal phase-to-ground or phase-to-phase transmission system voltage, unless the Planning Coordinator, Transmission Planner or Transmission Owner defines another | Attachment 1, item 4 |
| Frequency | At the high-side of the main power transformer, over a period of time, typically 3 to 6 cycles | Attachment 2, items 1 and 2 |

How this package works those quantities out from a recorder file is in
[RECORDINGS.md](RECORDINGS.md).

## How the rows are stored, and the reading in it

The standard prints each voltage row as a threshold. Attachment 1, item 7
says that where voltage keeps changing, "the duration within each band" is
added over any 10 second period. This package stores each row as a band that
runs from its own threshold to the next one. In Table 2 the row printed
"< 0.90" with 6.00 s is stored as 0.70 up to 0.90.

That reading is ours. The other reading takes each time as the limit on all
time spent below the threshold, and the two can disagree. Take Table 2 and a
sag of 0.60 per unit for 2.9 s followed by 0.80 per unit for 3.2 s. Counted
by bands it spends 2.9 s against 3.00 and 3.2 s against 6.00, so the
resource must ride through. Counted below each threshold it spends 6.1 s
below 0.90 against 6.00, so it may trip.

A threshold value itself goes to the row whose printed sign includes it.
"< 0.90" leaves 0.90 in the continuous row, and "< 0.70" leaves 0.70 in the
row printed "< 0.90".

Table 1 prints "≥ 1.10" for its 1.0 s row and Table 2 prints "> 1.10". Both
are stored as printed. At exactly 1.10 per unit Table 1 gives the mandatory
region and 1.0 s, and Table 2 gives the continuous region and 1800 s.

## The constants

| Constant | Value | Clause |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PRC029_MIN_MVA` | 20 MVA | Section 4.2.2 |
| `PRC029_MIN_KV` | 60 kV | Section 4.2.2 |
| `PRC029_PHASE_JUMP_DEGREES` | 25 electrical degrees | R1 |
| `PRC029_VHZ_PU`, `PRC029_VHZ_SECONDS` | 1.1 per unit for 45 s | R1 |
| `PRC029_VHZ_HIGH_PU`, `PRC029_VHZ_HIGH_SECONDS` | 1.18 per unit for 2 s | R1 |
| `PRC029_PRIORITY_BELOW_PU` | 0.95 per unit | R2.1.3 |
| `PRC029_RESTART_CYCLES` | 5 cycles | R2.3.1 |
| `PRC029_RECOVERY_SECONDS` | 1.0 s | R2.5 |
| `PRC029_ROCOF_HZ_PER_SECOND` | 5 Hz per second | R3 |
| `PRC029_ROCOF_MIN_PERIOD_SECONDS` | 0.1 s | Footnote 9 |
| `PRC029_VOLTAGE_WINDOW_SECONDS` | 10 s | Attachment 1, items 7 to 9 |
| `PRC029_MAX_DEVIATIONS` | 4 | Attachment 1, item 9 |
| `PRC029_FREQUENCY_WINDOW_SECONDS` | 600 s | Attachment 2, item 5 |

The phase jump and volts per hertz values are the conditions in R1 under
which ride-through is not required. The 10 second and 10 minute values are
the periods over which the standard makes its times cumulative. Item 9 lets
a resource trip for more than four deviations outside the continuous
operation region within any 10 second period.

## Checking

```
python validate/check_prc029.py
python validate/check_prc029.py --pdf prc-029-2.pdf
```

The first form parses the three tables out of `validate/src_prc_029_2.txt`,
works out from the printed signs which values each row covers, and asserts
the package returns that row's region and time at both ends of it. It then
asserts each constant against a sentence in the same file. It runs in CI.

The second form also checks the extract: every line of it must be found on
its stated page of the PDF you pass. It needs `pdftotext` from poppler.

## Not carried

This page covers the three tables and the constants above. The exemption
process in R4, the VSC-HVDC chopper provision in Attachment 1 item 12 and
the filtering rule in Attachment 1 item 10 are not carried.
