P15: 1. Table 1 applies to type 3 and type 4 wind Inverter-Based Resources or hybrid Inverter- Based Resources that include wind, unless connected via a dedicated Voltage Source Converter - High Voltage Direct Current (VSC-HVDC) transmission facility.
P15: 2. Table 2 applies to all other Inverter-Based Resource types not covered in Table 1; including, but not limited to, the following facilities: a. Inverter-Based Resources, regardless of their energy resource, interconnecting via a dedicated VSC-HVDC transmission facility. b. Other Inverter-Based Resource or hybrid Inverter-Based Resources consisting of photovoltaic (PV) and BESS.
P15: 4. The voltage base for per unit calculation is the nominal phase-to-ground or phase-to- phase transmission system voltage unless otherwise defined by the Planning Coordinator, Transmission Planner, or Transmission Owner.
P15: 5. The applicable voltage for Tables 1 and 2 is identified as the voltage max/min of phase- to-neutral or phase-to-phase fundamental root mean square (RMS) voltage at the high- side of the main power transformer.
P15: 6. Tables 1 and 2 are only applicable when the frequency is within the “must Ride-through zone” as specified in Figure 1 of Attachment 2.
P15: 7. At any given voltage value, each Inverter-Based Resources shall Ride-through unless the time duration at that voltage has exceeded the specified minimum Ride-through time duration. If the voltage is continuously varying over time, it is necessary to add the duration within each band of Tables 1 and 2 over any 10 second time period.
P15: 8. The specified duration of the mandatory operation regions and the permissive operation regions in Tables 1 and 2 is cumulative over one or more disturbances within any 10 second time period.
P15: 9. The Inverter-Based Resources may trip for more than four deviations of the applicable voltage at the high-side of the main power transformer outside of the continuous operation region within any 10 second time period.
P15: 11. The “must Ride-through zone” is the combined area of the mandatory operating regions, the continuous operating regions, and the permissive operating region. All area outside of these operating regions is referred to as the “may Ride-through zone.”
